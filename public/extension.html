Get the SameSecond extension

The extension keeps the video in sync on any site, including Netflix, Crunchyroll and other sites with protected video. Everyone in the room needs it on a laptop or PC, signed in to their own account on the site.

Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera

  1. Download and unzip

    Download the .zip above and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it, such as your Documents folder. The browser loads the extension from that folder, so don't delete it afterwards.

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar (edge://extensions in Edge, opera://extensions in Opera) and switch on Developer mode at the top right.

  3. Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked and choose the folder you unzipped (the one that contains manifest.json).

  4. Check it's there

    SameSecond appears in your list of extensions with its switch turned on.

  5. Pin the button

    Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar and pin SameSecond, so its button is always one click away. Clicking it opens SameSecond's panel at the side of the window.

Chrome may occasionally remind you that a developer-mode extension is installed. That's expected for extensions installed this way; keep it on.

Firefox on a computer or Android

Install SameSecond from Firefox Add-ons with one click (it appears there once Mozilla finishes its review). On Android, open that link in Firefox, or open Firefox's menu, then Add-ons, and search for SameSecond. On Firefox, the panel opens in the sidebar.

Some sites, including Netflix, don't play in phone browsers at all. On those, use your phone for chat and voice from the invite link instead.
