Syncroom

Get the Syncroom extension

The extension syncs video on any site, including Netflix, Crunchyroll and other sites with protected video. Everyone needs it, signed in to their own account on the site.

Download

The extension is set up for this site, so rooms you start land here.

Download syncroom-extension.zip

Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera

  1. Download syncroom-extension.zip above and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin Syncroom from the puzzle-piece menu so its button is always visible.

Firefox on a computer

  1. Install Syncroom from addons.mozilla.org once it's listed there. Firefox only installs extensions that Mozilla has signed.
  2. When Firefox asks, allow Syncroom to access websites. You can also do this from the Syncroom sidebar.

Firefox on Android

  1. Install Firefox from the Play Store, then install Syncroom from its add-ons page once it's listed there.
  2. Open the video's page, tap the menu, then Add-ons → Syncroom.
  3. Some sites, including Netflix, don't play in phone browsers at all. On those, use your phone for chat and voice from the invite link instead.

iPhone and other Android browsers

These can't run the extension. Open the invite link to watch YouTube and video-link rooms, or to chat and talk while you watch on another screen.
